Home sound alerts.

If you're deaf or hard of hearing, wear headphones a lot, or work in a far room, it's easy to miss the doorbell, the smoke alarm or the baby. Teach this page a sound with a few recordings. While it listens, it flashes the screen in that sound's colour, vibrates and logs the time when it hears it again.

    Teach a new sound

    Then press Record on it and make the sound 2 to 5 times, one recording each. Each recording lasts 3 seconds.

    Higher catches quieter or more distant sounds but gives more false alarms. In test mode, the bars show the live match and nothing flashes, so you can tune this.

    Log
    • Nothing heard yet.

    Each recording becomes a fingerprint: how loud the sound is in 40 pitch bands spaced like human hearing, averaged over the sound, plus the shape of its loudness over time. While listening, the page compares the last moment of audio with every fingerprint using cosine similarity, a score from 0 to 1 for how alike two patterns are.

    Be honest with yourself about the limits. This tab must stay open and the screen on; it asks your device to keep the screen awake, but phones may still stop the microphone when you switch apps or lock the screen. It can miss sounds or alert on similar ones. It is not a certified alarm and doesn't replace a working smoke alarm or a visual doorbell. Audio never leaves your device; only the fingerprints are saved in this browser.
