IP & VPN leak test.

Every website you visit sees your IP address, and from it, roughly where you are and who your internet provider is. A VPN is supposed to hide that. Here's what sites see right now, and whether your real address is slipping out some other way.

How to use it: run it once with your VPN off and note the provider, then switch the VPN on and run again. Everything should change to the VPN's location and company. If your home provider (for example e& or du) still appears anywhere, something is leaking. Your IP is sent to ipify, ipwho.is and a Google STUN server to look it up; this page stores nothing.
