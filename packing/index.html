Tool · Travel · UAE

Smart packing list.

Tell it where you're going, when, and what you'll do there. It checks the weather for those dates, works out how many of each thing you need, and adds the reminders people flying out of the UAE tend to forget. Tick things off as you pack; your list stays on this device.

    How the numbers work: without laundry you pack one outfit a day, up to two weeks. With laundry once or twice it caps at a week, and with laundry whenever you like, four days. Socks and underwear get one spare. Weather comes from Open-Meteo: the forecast if your trip starts within two weeks, otherwise last year's weather for the same dates as a rough guide.
