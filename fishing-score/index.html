Fishing now.

Anglers on the Corniche and the breakwaters will tell you fish bite when the water is moving, at first and last light, and around the new and full moon. This puts those rules together with the tide, moon, pressure and wind forecast for your spot and picks the best three windows in the next 48 hours.

Best three windows
    Licence and rules. Fishing in the UAE, even from the shore, needs a recreational fishing licence from the Ministry of Climate Change and Environment (MOCCAE) or your emirate's environment authority. There are also rules on species, sizes, areas and seasons. Check the current rules with the authority before you go.

    What the score rewards. Water moving in the two hours or so either side of a high or low tide (bait gets stirred up). Solunar periods: the moon overhead or underfoot, plus or minus an hour (major), and moonrise and moonset, plus or minus 30 minutes (minor). An hour either side of sunrise and sunset. Steady or slowly falling pressure. Wind under 15 knots. And the few days around a new or full moon, when tides run strongest.

    What's approximate. Tides come from the Open-Meteo marine model, not a harbour gauge, so turns can be 30 minutes or more off, more so inside creeks and lagoons. Moon times are computed in your browser from a low-precision model (within a few minutes). Solunar theory is folklore with mixed evidence. Treat the score as a guide to when to go, not a promise of fish. Check the weather and sea state before going out on the water.
