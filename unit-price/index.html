    Aisle tips
    • Check the small print on the shelf label. Many supermarkets in the UAE already print the price per kg or per litre in small type under the main price.
    • Bulk isn't always cheaper. A big pack at full price can cost more per kg than a smaller pack on offer.
    • Watch the drained weight on canned food. A 400 g tin of chickpeas may hold only 240 g once the water is gone. Compare the drained weight.
    • A deal only counts if you use it all. Three bottles of oil for the price of two is no saving if one goes off.
    • Rolls and sheets don't compare. One roll can hold 150 sheets or 300. Use the sheet count from the pack.

    How offers are counted. "Buy 2 get 1 free" means 3 packs for the price of 2, so each pack costs a third less. "Extra 20% free" sounds similar, but it is only about 17% off, because you pay the same for 1.2 times as much. A straight "20% off" is the better of the two.
