      Why GPS fails underground. Satellite signals are weak, about the strength of a car headlight seen from 20,000 km away. Concrete blocks them, so in a basement your phone guesses from Wi-Fi and the last fix, and the accuracy circle grows past 50 metres. That's the cue this page uses to ask for your level.

      Step counting uses the phone's accelerometer to spot each footfall and the compass for direction. Each step is taken as about 70 cm. Errors add up, and steel beams can bend the compass, so treat the trail as a rough shape, not a map. Your photo of the pillar sign is the most reliable clue.

      Everything stays on this phone: spots in local storage, photos in the browser's own database. Nothing is uploaded.
