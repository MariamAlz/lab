Plant care for UAE homes.

Most plant advice is written for cool, rainy countries. Here a pot on a sunny balcony can dry out in a day in July, while the same plant indoors under AC might need water once a week. This page follows the real weather where you live to say when to water, works out how much a garden needs, and helps diagnose a struggling plant.

How it works: each day the weather service estimates how much water evaporates from soil and leaves (evapotranspiration). The page multiplies that by the plant's thirst and its spot, and compares it with how much water the pot can hold. In summer outdoors, small pots can need water twice a day.
