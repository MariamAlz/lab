Rental view checker.

The listing says "sea view". Before you sign, tap the building on the map and pick the floor. This looks out in every direction from that height, using the footprints and heights of the buildings around it. It shows which way you'll face a wall, where you'll see the sea or water, and whether the sunset is in view.

Rough guide only. OpenStreetMap height data is incomplete. Many buildings have no height or floor count, so they get a default of 4 floors, and new towers may be missing entirely. Visit at the time of day you care about before you commit.

From the chosen height, a line is traced every 2° around the compass, 180 lines in all. Each line checks every building edge it crosses. A building blocks the view if its roof is above your eye level. Sea counts as visible if the line reaches the coastline before anything taller than you. In the UAE the sun sets in the west-southwest in December (around 243°) and the west-northwest in June (around 297°).

Building data comes from OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API. Heights use the height tag, then floor count times your floor height, then a 4-floor guess. Only buildings within 700 m are checked. Beyond that, a "sea" result means the coast is in that direction and nothing nearby blocks it, not that it's guaranteed.
