Portal upload fixer.

Government and visa portals often reject a file for being 1.4 MB when the limit is 500 KB, or for being an iPhone HEIC photo. Drop your photos or PDFs here, pick the limit, and get files that fit. Nothing is uploaded: the shrinking happens on your device.

How it hits the limit: a JPG has a quality dial from 0 to 100. The page saves your photo at the middle setting, checks the size, then halves the gap again and again (a binary search). Seven tries finds the best quality that still fits. If even low quality is too big, it makes the picture a bit smaller and tries again.

To be safe it counts 1 KB as 1000 bytes, the stricter reading, and aims a little under. Saving a photo again also removes its hidden location and camera data.

PDFs

PDFs are shrunk by turning each page into a JPG picture. Text will no longer be selectable, which portals don't mind. Keep the original. To merge or reorder PDFs first, use PDF tools.
