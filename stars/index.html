Brightest things up now

    Arab astronomers and navigators named hundreds of stars, and many of those names came into European star atlases almost unchanged: Aldebaran is الدبران, Altair is الطائر. "Desert sky" adds a sprinkle of faint stars and the Milky Way as an illustration; the named stars are real positions.
