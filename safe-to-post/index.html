Safe to post.

Before you share a photo, cover what a stranger shouldn't see: faces, number plates, documents, addresses and screens. This page finds the obvious ones for you, lets you add or remove boxes, and saves a clean copy with the location and camera data removed. Your photo never leaves this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop a photo here, paste one, or choose a file.

Cover with:

Drag on the photo to add a box. Tap a box to switch it on or off. Dashed boxes stay visible.

    Save a clean copy
    • The saved copy is redrawn from scratch, so location, camera and date data are not carried over.

    Saved as a JPEG at the same size, up to 3,000 pixels on the long side.

    Why there is no blur

    Light blur and small pixel blocks can be partly undone, and blurred text can be guessed by blurring likely words and comparing. A solid box removes the pixels for good. Big pixels here use very large blocks, at most five across a face and one per line of text, which is much harder to reverse. A solid box is still the safest.

    Also check for

    How it works: faces are found by MediaPipe's face detector and text by Tesseract, both running in your browser. Lines that are mostly digits are treated as plates, IDs or phone numbers. The first run downloads about 10 MB of models, then your browser keeps them.
