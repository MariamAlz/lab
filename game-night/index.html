Game night.

One phone in the middle of the table for everything a box usually loses: dice of any kind, whose turn it is, a turn timer or chess clock for slow thinkers, a score sheet that adds itself up, and a fair way to pick who goes first. It keeps working without a connection, and remembers the game if you close it.

The chosen player also becomes the first turn above.

Are the dice fair? Each roll uses your browser's cryptographic random generator (crypto.getRandomValues) with rejection sampling, so every face is exactly equally likely. Plastic dice are rarely that good: cheap ones with drilled pips are slightly lighter on the 6 side, so 6 comes up a little more often.

Chess clock mode gives each player a bank of time that only runs on their turn. It is good for games where one slow thinker holds up everyone else. Turn timer mode gives the same short countdown every turn, which suits party games. Everything is saved on this device only.
