    What it says

    This works by matching about 30 kinds of phrases, like "share with our partners" or "مقابل ذي قيمة", in both languages. It doesn't understand the text the way a lawyer does, so it can miss clauses written in unusual words, and a sentence like "we never sell your data" is shown as a good sign rather than a warning.

    In the UAE, the Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) gives people rights over their personal data, such as access and correction, and sets rules for sending it outside the country. Free zones like DIFC and ADGM have their own data protection laws.

    This is a reading aid, not legal advice. For anything important, read the full policy and ask a qualified adviser.
