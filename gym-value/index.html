VisitsBreak-even visits a month

    The sunk cost trap. Once you've paid for a year, the money is gone whether you go or not. So "I paid, I should go" is a good nudge, but "I paid, so it was worth it" isn't. The honest number is the cost per visit you actually make. A membership only beats day passes once you pass the break-even line.

    A month here is 4.35 weeks (52 weeks ÷ 12). A free freeze moves your unused months to the end, so you still get them. Months away with no freeze are paid for but not used. Before you sign, check the notice period, auto-renewal and what happens if you move. This is an estimate to help you decide, not financial advice.
