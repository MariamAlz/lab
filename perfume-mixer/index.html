        Why heavy goes first. Base notes such as oud, amber, sandalwood and musk are made of larger, slower molecules that stay on skin for hours. Bright notes like citrus evaporate in minutes. Put the heavy one on first, and the light one on top, so you still smell the top note when you walk out of the door instead of it being buried.

        The harmony score comes from a handful of simple rules about which scent families tend to sit well together, plus classic pairings like rose with oud. It's a starting point for your own nose, not a perfumer's verdict. Test new oils on a small patch of skin first, and keep strong oils off silk and white fabric.
