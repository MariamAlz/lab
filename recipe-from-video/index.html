Recipe from a video.

Cooking videos move fast and never write anything down. Save one from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube to your phone, drop it here, and a speech recognition model running on your own device listens to it. Then this page pulls out the ingredients, amounts, times and steps into a recipe you can keep, scale and print.

How it works

The first time, your browser downloads Whisper, an open speech recognition model (about 80 MB), and keeps it for next time. The video never leaves your phone. A minute of video takes around a minute to transcribe on a phone, and faster on a laptop.

Videos with only music and on-screen text have no speech to hear. For those, paste the caption instead. The recipe is worked out with simple rules, not a chatbot, so check the amounts before you cook. Everything stays editable.
