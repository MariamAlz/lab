    Numbers show the ranking. The white dot is you.

    Why the mountains are cooler. Air cools by about 6.5°C for every 1,000 m you climb, because rising air expands and loses heat as it does. The viewing deck on Jebel Jais sits around 1,650 m, so it's often 10°C cooler than the coast. Weather models smooth mountains into gentle hills, so this page asks for each forecast at the real height of the place you'd stand.

    How the score works. Each place gets 100 points, minus points for whatever spoils the plan. For cool air: heat and sticky humidity. For swimming: sea temperature away from about 27°C, wind that makes waves, and heat on the sand. For hiking: heat over 26°C, humidity and strong UV. For camping: a warm or windy night, or rain.

    Forecasts from Open-Meteo, sea temperature from the Open-Meteo marine model. Drive times are rough, without traffic. Mountain weather changes fast: check road closures and wadi warnings from the NCM before you go, and never enter a flowing wadi.
