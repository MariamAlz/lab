Sun path on your balcony.

Stand at your window or on your balcony and point your phone out. You'll see the sun's path today drawn over the view, along with its highest path in June and lowest in December. That tells you where shade falls, where plants will thrive or burn, and whether a solar panel or a new curtain is worth it.

Your phone will ask for the camera, motion sensors and location. Nothing leaves your device.

Reading the result

For plants: 6 hours or more of sun is "full sun" (bougainvillea, frangipani, herbs in winter). 3 to 6 hours is "part sun", good for most balcony plants. Under 3 hours suits shade lovers such as pothos and peace lilies. In a UAE summer, afternoon sun from the west is the harshest, so even sun-loving plants want shade after 2 pm.

For cooling: windows facing west or south-west take the most heat in the hot months. Shade or reflective film on them saves the most on cooling bills. North-facing windows get almost no direct sun and stay cooler.

Paths are calculated for your location (Abu Dhabi if you don't share it) and ignore buildings around you. That's what the camera is for: you can see whether a tower blocks part of the path.
