Dhow navigator.

Sail from Julfar, today's Ras Al Khaimah, with no GPS and no clock good enough to tell your longitude. What you do have is الجدي, the Pole Star, and a kamal: a small board on a knotted string that measures how high it stands. Its height is your latitude.

Ahmad ibn Majid, born in Julfar in the 1400s, wrote one of the great guides to sailing by the stars. Navigators measured star heights in isba‘ (finger widths, about 1.6°), and sailed north or south to a port's latitude first, then ran east or west along it until land appeared. In his day the Pole Star sat a few degrees from the true pole and needed a correction; here it's exact, to keep things simple.
