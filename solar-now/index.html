A typical year (kWh per month)

Dust is the UAE's biggest hidden solar loss. A thin film of fine sand blocks light before it reaches the cells, and in humid mornings dew turns it into a crust that wind can't blow away. Studies in the Gulf often report losses of roughly 0.3 to 1% a day on panels that are never cleaned, so a month without cleaning can cost 10% or more, and one dust storm can do that overnight. That's why the soiling setting matters, and why big UAE solar parks clean their panels with robots every few days.

Heat is the second loss. Panels are rated at 25°C, but on a UAE summer roof the cells run at 60 to 70°C, and each degree above 25 costs about 0.35% of output. So a clear day in January can beat a clear day in July, even though the July sun is higher.

An estimate from a simple model and a weather forecast, not a quote. For a real system, ask a licensed installer (Shams Dubai in Dubai, or your emirate's utility scheme) for a site survey.

Your system

A typical villa roof fits 5 to 10 kWp. In the UAE a tilt of about 20 to 25° facing south catches the most over a year. Soiling: about 2–3% if cleaned weekly, 5% fortnightly, 10–20% after a month or a dust storm.
