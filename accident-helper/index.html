      The accident

      5 · What happens next
      Get the police reportInsurers and garages in the UAE generally need a police report before any repair, even for a scratch. Keep the report number. The app usually sends it by SMS or email.
      Tell your insurer quicklyMost policies ask you to report within a set number of days. Send the police report, photos, your licence, registration (mulkiya) and the other driver's details.
      If you were not at faultThe claim usually goes to the other driver's insurer. They arrange the repair at an approved garage or agency, depending on their policy.
      If you were at faultYour own insurer handles it. With third-party-only cover, your own repair is usually not covered. An at-fault claim can affect your no-claims discount at renewal.
      Repairs without a report are riskyGarages are not supposed to fix accident damage without a report, and an unreported dent can cause trouble at the next registration test or sale.
      Good to know

      Rules and apps differ by emirate and change from time to time. As of 2026, the UAE government portal lists the Abu Dhabi Police, Dubai Police, Rafid (Sharjah), MOI UAE and Saaed apps for reporting minor accidents. Check the official police website or app in your emirate for the current process and any fees.

      Why move the car? A stopped car in a live lane causes a large share of secondary crashes. Abu Dhabi Police reminded drivers in 2024 that stopping in the road without need after a minor accident can bring a Dh1,000 fine. If anyone is hurt, the car can't be driven, or you can't agree, stay put with hazards on and call 999.
