    Where the data goes. Video is almost all of it. An hour of standard video is about 0.7 GB, and HD can use 2 GB or more. An hour of maps or music uses a tiny fraction of that. So the two settings that matter most are video quality on mobile data and how much of your watching happens on Wi-Fi.

    The habit estimates assume, per hour on mobile data: video 0.3 / 0.7 / 2 GB (data saver / standard / HD), short-video social apps 0.7 GB, video calls 0.5 GB, browsing and chat 0.15 GB, music 0.07 GB, app voice calls 0.04 GB, maps 0.02 GB. Real use varies by app and screen. Check your phone's data counter after a month. Offers change often, so this is a guide, not financial advice.
