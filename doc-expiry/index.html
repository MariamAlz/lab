Emirates IDs, passports, visas, licences, Mulkiya, insurance, Ejari: a family of four can easily have twenty dates to remember, and a missed one means fines or a blocked renewal. Put them all here, see what's coming up on one timeline, and send reminders to your calendar 30 and 7 days before each one.

    A small trick that saves a lot of hassle: many UAE renewals depend on each other. Health insurance must be valid to renew a residence visa, the visa renews the Emirates ID, and car insurance must be valid before the Mulkiya can be renewed. So when one date is close, check the ones it depends on too.
