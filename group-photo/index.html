A group photo that waits for open eyes.

In every family photo someone blinks. Set how many people are in the shot and prop up your phone. The camera watches every face and takes the picture only when everyone's eyes are open and they're facing the lens. The photos stay on your phone.

Looking for faces…

Your browser will ask to use the camera. The face model (about 4 MB) downloads once. Nothing you film is uploaded.

Settings Your photos

Photos you take appear here. Tap Download to save one.

The face model finds 478 points on each face and scores 52 expressions, including how closed each eye is and where the eyes are looking. A blink scores above about 0.5. The camera also checks that each nose is roughly centred between the cheeks, which means the head is turned to the lens.

It's approximate. Glasses with glare, very small faces at the back of a big group, strong backlight and people in profile can confuse it. Use the burst to get a few to choose from, and keep the faces reasonably large in the frame.
