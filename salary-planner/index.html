Where your take-home pay goes
    50/30/20 check

    A popular rule of thumb: about half on needs, under a third on wants, at least a fifth saved. The white line marks the target. In the UAE rent alone often pushes needs past 50%, which is why many people aim higher on savings in other years to catch up.

    A planning aid, not financial advice. For decisions about loans, investments or pensions, talk to a licensed adviser.

    Income (per month)

    There is no personal income tax in the UAE. UAE nationals contribute to the pension fund: 11% for people who joined on or after October 2023 (GPSSA) or December 2023 (ADPF), 5% for many who joined earlier, worked out on basic plus fixed allowances and capped at AED 70,000 a month for GPSSA. Check your payslip and edit the percentage to match.
