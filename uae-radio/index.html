UAE radio live.

The UAE has radio in a dozen languages, from Quran and Arabic talk to English pop, Hindi film songs and Malayalam news. This lists the stations streaming online, most popular first, so you can listen in your browser, keep favourites and fall asleep to one with a timer.

    Why there's no song title. Radio streams slip the song name into the audio data every few seconds (called ICY metadata), but browsers don't let a web page read it. So this shows what the station says about itself: its genre tags, language and bitrate. 128 kbps sounds about as good as FM; 64 kbps is fine for talk.

    What's approximate. The list comes from radio-browser.info, a free directory that anyone can edit, so a few names, languages or logos may be off, and stations change their stream links without warning. Only stations with a secure (https) stream can play on this page. The sound wave appears only when a station allows it.
