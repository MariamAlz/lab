Is your home in your running routes?

Most runs and rides start and end at your front door. Post enough of them and the map points straight at your home, and often at your office, with the times you leave and come back. Load your own activity files to see what they reveal, how much a privacy zone really hides, and save trimmed copies.

🔒Your routes never leave this device. Files are read inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded. Only map tiles are loaded from the internet.

Settings to check in your apps
  • Default visibility. Set new activities to followers only or private. In most apps this is under privacy or social settings.
  • Hide start and end. Many apps (Strava, Garmin Connect, Komoot, Suunto, Polar and others) can hide the first and last part of every route, or hide a zone around an address. Turn it on for home and work.
  • Pick a zone centre that isn't your door. A zone centred exactly on your home can be found again from where routes cross its edge. Centre it a few streets away, or use a large radius.
  • Check older activities. New settings may not apply to activities posted before. Look for an option to apply them to past activities.
  • Leaderboards, heatmaps and "people nearby". Opt out of public segments, heatmap contributions and flyby features if you don't need them.
  • Linked accounts. Watches and phone health apps can sync to several services. Each one needs its own privacy settings.
  • Vary where you start. Start recording a few hundred metres from home, or drive to a different start sometimes.

How it works. The page takes the first and last point of every activity and groups points that are within 150 m of each other (a method called DBSCAN). A spot where many activities begin or end, at the same hours, is almost always a home or a workplace. The times of day and days of the week show your routine.

Why zones leak. A privacy zone hides the route inside a circle, but every route still appears at the circle's edge. With a few routes, the points where they appear sit on that circle, and the centre of the circle is your door. That is why apps now shift zone centres randomly, and why a bigger zone helps.
