    Three sounds that give Emirati away. The letter ق (q) is usually said as g, so qahwa (coffee) becomes gahwa. In many words ك (k) becomes ch, as in chaif for "how". And ج (j) often becomes y, so dajaj (chicken) becomes diyay. Speech also changes between emirates, between town and desert families, and between generations.

    Tags. Emirati marks dialect or Gulf words you would not normally find in formal Arabic. Shared marks words that are the same in formal Arabic (MSA) or across the Arab world. Where it differs, "to a man / to a woman" forms are given. The device voice reads the Arabic script in a fairly standard accent, so trust the transliteration for the local sound.

    Transliteration key: kh as in Scottish loch, gh a soft gargled g, ʿ a tight sound from the throat (ع), dh as in "this", th as in "think", a long vowel is doubled (aa, ee, oo).
