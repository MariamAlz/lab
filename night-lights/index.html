The UAE at night.

Every night a NASA and NOAA satellite passes over the UAE at about 1:30 in the morning and photographs the lights below. Pick a year for each side, then drag the gold handle to swipe between them. New islands, roads, ports and whole neighbourhoods show up as fresh lines of light.

    About the images

    2012 and 2016 are NASA's Black Marble composites. Each one stitches together the clearest, moonless nights of a whole year and removes clouds, so they are the fairest comparison. Other years show a single night close to the October new moon, when there is no moonlight and the sky over the UAE is usually clear. A single night can still have cloud, haze or a few missing strips.

    The satellite's Day/Night Band is sensitive enough to see one street lamp's worth of light from 830 km up. Single nights and yearly composites use different brightness scales, so compare where lights are, not exactly how bright. Use the date box below to try any other night.
