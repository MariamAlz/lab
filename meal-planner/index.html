Weekly meal planner.

Fill a week of breakfasts, lunches and dinners from about 40 everyday home dishes, from machboos and harees to shakshuka and lentil soup, or add your own. The shopping list builds itself, adds up the same ingredient across meals and sorts it by supermarket section. Send it to WhatsApp or print it.

    Start with an amount and a unit (g, kg, ml, l, pcs, tbsp, tsp, cup, bunch, pack, can). The section is guessed; add @produce, @meat, @dairy, @bakery, @pantry, @spices or @frozen to set it.

    Shopping list
    WhatsApp

    Amounts are scaled from each recipe's usual serving count to the number of people eating, then added together. Grams and kilos are combined, so two dishes needing 500 g and 1.2 kg of chicken show as 1.7 kg. Things sold whole, like onions, bunches of parsley or packs of bread, are rounded up. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
