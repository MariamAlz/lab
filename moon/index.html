Where's the Moon?

The Moon right now, tilted the way you'd see it from Abu Dhabi (or from wherever you are). The little map shows why it has that shape: half of it is always lit by the Sun, and we see that half from a changing angle.

The positions use a simplified version of the standard lunar theory, accurate to a few arcminutes, which is plenty for rise times and phase. Crescent visibility is a rough guide. In the UAE, the start of each Hijri month is announced by the official moon-sighting committee.
