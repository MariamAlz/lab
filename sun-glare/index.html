      Glare here means the sun is less than 25° above the horizon and within 25° of the way you're facing. A car visor usually covers the sky above about 15 to 20°, so the worst glare is the lowest sun. In the UAE, roads running east and west get it in spring and autumn. Roads running southwest, like the E11 to Abu Dhabi, get it on winter afternoons.

      This is approximate. Each leg is a straight line between the points you set, so curves, flyovers and buildings or trees that shade the road are ignored. Trip timing assumes a steady speed. Times are Gulf Standard Time. On a bad day, leaving 15 minutes earlier or later often fixes it, and sunglasses and a clean windscreen help a lot.
