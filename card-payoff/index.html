Why the minimum takes so long: when the minimum is a percentage of what you owe, it shrinks as the balance shrinks. At 5% of the balance and 3.25% interest, only about 1.7% of the debt goes away each month, so the last part drags on for years.

      Both methods pay every card's minimum and put the rest of your budget on one card. Avalanche picks the highest rate first, which always costs the least interest. Snowball picks the smallest balance first, so a card disappears sooner, and many people find that easier to stick with.

      Balance transfers and instalment plans

      Many UAE banks offer to move a balance to a 0% or low-rate plan for a few months, or to split a big purchase into instalments. They can save a lot, but check three things first: the one-time processing fee, the rate once the offer ends, and whether new spending on the card is still charged the full rate. Work out the fee as a monthly cost and compare it with the rate above.

      This is an estimate, not financial advice. Banks charge interest daily and add late fees, so your statement will differ a little. If repayments are hard, talk to your bank early.
