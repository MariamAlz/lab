Good to know

A tola is 11.6638 grams, three-eighths of a troy ounce. Gold is priced per troy ounce (31.10 g), not the everyday ounce (28.35 g). The 10-tola TT bar sold in Dubai's souks weighs 116.64 g.

A UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 litres, a US gallon 3.785. Older car brochures from the region often quote miles per imperial gallon. An AC ton isn't a weight: it's the cooling power of melting one ton of ice a day, 12,000 BTU an hour or about 3.5 kW of heat moved. A typical bedroom split unit is 1.5 to 2 tons.

The dirham has been pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725 since 1997, so this rate doesn't move. Gold value is spot price only; shops add making charges and VAT.
