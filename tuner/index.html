Tool · Sound · Music

Instrument tuner.

Pluck a string and your microphone finds its pitch. Most tuners only know the 12 Western notes. This one also knows the quarter tones of Arabic maqam, like the half-flat E in Rast and Bayati, so you can check those notes too.

—
Press start, then play a single string.
Settings

Arabic oud tuning (low to high): C F A D G C. Turkish oud: E A B E A D. In Arabic practice a quarter tone sits halfway between two semitones, about 50 cents. Players bend it slightly by maqam and region, so trust your ear too.

Quarter tones you'll meet

Rast (راست): C D E half-flat F G A B half-flat C. Bayati (بياتي): D E half-flat F G A B♭ C D. Sikah (سيكاه) starts on E half-flat itself. Saba (صبا): D E half-flat F G♭ A B♭ C. Play one of these notes and the tuner should read "half-flat" at the centre.

Pitch is found on your device by autocorrelation: the sound wave is compared with shifted copies of itself to find how often it repeats. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
