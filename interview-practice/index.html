Why STAR? Interviewers who ask "tell me about a time when…" are scoring evidence, not opinions. Situation, Task, Action, Result keeps a story short and puts the weight on what you did. Aim for about 10% situation, 10% task, 60% action and 20% result. Most good answers land between one and two minutes.

Calm speech in an interview is usually around 120 to 160 words per minute. Faster than that often comes from nerves. A few fillers like "um" are normal; it is long strings of "so, basically, like" that distract. A short pause sounds more confident than a filler.
