Spoof or real?

Navigation signals are faint, so they can be drowned out (jamming) or imitated (spoofing). You're watching a receiver in a car. Read its signals and call it: clean, jammed or spoofed. The sooner you're right, the more you score.

Six drives

Each drive lasts two minutes, played fast. Some are fine. Some are not.

  2. 02Watch for changes that hit every satellite at once, a clock that jumps, or a position that leaves the road.
  3. 03Calling an attack too early is a false alarm. Calling "clean" before the end is a gamble.

This is simulated and kept simple, but these tells are the real starting points for interference monitoring, the kind of thing GNSS engineers build tools for.
