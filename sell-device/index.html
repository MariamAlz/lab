Before you sell your phone or laptop.

A factory reset takes one tap. Doing it in the wrong order can lock you out of your bank, your authenticator codes or your WhatsApp chats, or leave the buyer with a device that's locked to your account. Pick the device and work down the list. Your ticks are saved on this device.

Why the order matters

Authenticator apps keep their secret keys only on the phone. Once you erase it, those six-digit codes are gone for good, and getting back into each account means slow identity checks with every service. Activation Lock and Android's Factory Reset Protection work the other way round: they survive the reset on purpose, to make stolen phones useless. That's why you sign out first, or the buyer ends up with a locked device and you get a call.

Good to know
  • Menu names change between software versions. If a path doesn't match, search the Settings app for the key word.
  • Modern phones encrypt everything. A proper reset throws away the key, so "special wiping apps" aren't needed.
  • Selling a work or school device? It isn't yours to wipe. Ask the IT team first.
  • Nothing on this page is sent anywhere. The ticks live in this browser only.
