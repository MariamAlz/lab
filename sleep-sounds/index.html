Sleep sounds mixer.

Build your own background for sleeping, napping or calming down: rain on the window, waves, a desert night with wind and crickets, a fan, a campfire. None of it is recorded. Every sound is generated live by your browser, so it never loops, and the sleep timer fades it out slowly instead of cutting off.

    How the sounds are made. Almost everything starts as noise: random numbers played as sound. White noise has equal energy at every pitch and sounds like hiss. Filtering it so lower pitches are louder gives pink noise (steady rain) and brown noise (a deep rumble, like distant surf). Waves are brown noise that swells and fades every 6 to 12 seconds. Crickets are a 4,500 Hz tone switched on and off in quick pulses, the way a real cricket scrapes its wings. Campfire crackles are tiny random bursts of filtered noise, a few milliseconds long.

    Tips. Keep the volume low: about the level of a quiet conversation is plenty, and well under the 60% mark on most phones. Some phones pause web audio when the screen locks. If that happens, leave the screen on at the lowest brightness, or plug in. Sound for sleep helps many people, but if you often can't sleep, talk to a doctor.
