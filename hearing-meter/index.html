How loud are everyday places?

    The 3 dB rule. Decibels are logarithmic: every extra 3 dB is twice the sound energy. So the safe time halves with every 3 dB. The US workplace safety body NIOSH sets 85 dB(A) for 8 hours as the limit. At 88 it's 4 hours, at 100 just 15 minutes, and at 110 (a loud concert) under 2 minutes. Damage adds up across the day, which is what the dose tile shows.

    Why "A"? Your ears are less sensitive to deep bass and very high sounds. A-weighting filters the microphone signal the same way, turning down low and very high frequencies, so the number matches what harms hearing. This page builds that filter from the standard's equations and runs it live on your microphone.

    An estimate, not a certified meter. Phone mics are tuned for speech and may clip above about 100 dB. Use it to compare and to spot patterns. For a formal noise complaint, the times and patterns in your log help, but your building management or municipality may want their own measurement. If your ears ring or sound muffled after noise, give them a rest, and see a doctor if it doesn't clear within a day or two.
