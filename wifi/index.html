Wi-Fi spot planner.

Wi-Fi fades with distance and loses a chunk at every wall, a lot more at concrete than at glass. Sketch your home, drop the router where it is now, and see roughly where the signal dies. Then drag it around, or let the planner find the best spot for a booster.

Router: drag the gold dot. Boosters: teal dots; double-tap one to remove it. Walls: drag to draw; they snap straight.

This is a simple model: free-space loss plus a fixed loss per wall (light wall 3 dB, block 8, concrete 13, glass 3, metal 25). Real homes add reflections and furniture, so treat it as a guide. 5 and 6 GHz are faster but weaker through walls than 2.4 GHz. Your plan is kept on this device.
