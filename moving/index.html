Moving home in the UAE.

Moving here has a strict order: no registered tenancy, no electricity; no electricity account, often no move-in permit. This page builds a dated plan from eight weeks before your move to two weeks after, with the right authority names for each emirate and the documents each step asks for. Tick things off as you go, add it to your calendar, or print it.

Your move

How it works: each step is tied to the steps it needs. A utility account in the UAE is opened against your registered tenancy (or title deed), and building management usually wants both before it issues a move-in permit. So registration sits early, and everything else hangs off it.
