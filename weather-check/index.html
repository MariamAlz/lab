Was the weather really like that?

A video of a flooded street goes round, labelled "today". Was it even raining? Pick the place and time, and this pulls the hourly weather record, dust levels and the sun's position for that moment, then checks what the photo or video shows against it.

    How good is this record? The weather comes from reanalysis: computer models blended with observations from weather stations, balloons and satellites. Each value covers a grid square about 9 to 25 km wide, so a short, local storm or a single flooded street can be missed, and coastal fog or a dust plume may be placed a few kilometres off. "Can't tell" is often the honest answer.

    Dust comes from the CAMS atmospheric model through the Open-Meteo air quality API, which only has history from about August 2022. Before that, dust shows as unavailable. The newest few days may be missing too, because the archive runs about 5 days behind.

    Shadows are worked out from the sun's height. At 20° above the horizon, a shadow is nearly three times as long as the person casting it. At 70°, it's a third. Compare that with the shadows in the picture. Also check the shadows point away from the sun's direction shown here.

    Weather data: Open-Meteo (ERA5 and ECMWF IFS reanalysis), CAMS. Sun position calculated in your browser.
