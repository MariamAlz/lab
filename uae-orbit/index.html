UAE in orbit.

Every satellite the UAE owns that's still in orbit, where it is this second, and where it's heading. The low ones circle the Earth every hour and a half; the high ones hang over the same spot on the equator.

Pick a satellite

Tap one on the map or in the list.

    The low satellites (KhalifaSat, MBZ-SAT and smaller ones) image the Earth or test new technology. Defence satellites aren't shown. The high ones, 36,000 km up, carry TV, internet and satellite phone calls across the region. Positions come from public orbital elements refreshed every six hours.
