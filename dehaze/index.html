Dehaze your own photo.

Underwater and foggy photos lose contrast because light scatters before it reaches the camera. Drop in a photo and this page estimates how much haze sits on every pixel, then removes it. Drag across the image to compare before and after.

How it works: the dark channel prior (He et al.) finds the haze, a guided filter keeps edges sharp, and a per-channel stretch removes the blue-green cast of underwater shots. My master's thesis at Khalifa University went further, with spiking GANs that run on low-power neuromorphic hardware.
