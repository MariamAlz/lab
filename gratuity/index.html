End-of-service gratuity.

Leaving a private-sector job in the UAE after at least a year? The law (Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021) owes you a gratuity based on your basic salary. Enter your dates and see the amount, with every step of the sum shown.

Under the 2021 law it no longer matters whether you resign or are let go, or whether your contract was limited or unlimited: the gratuity is the same. Only the basic salary counts, not housing or transport allowances.
