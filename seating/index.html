Seating planner.

Seating a wedding, a majlis or a big family dinner is a puzzle: families want to sit together, some people are best kept apart, and every table has a limit. Add your guests, tables and rules, press Arrange, then drag people around until it feels right. Separate halls or sections are supported too.

          Rules

            How Arrange works. It first seats whole families and must-sit pairs, biggest first, at the table with the most room. Then it runs simulated annealing: thousands of small random moves and swaps, keeping any change that lowers a penalty score and sometimes accepting a worse one early on, so it doesn't get stuck. Must-sit and keep-apart rules cost a lot, wishes and split groups cost a little.
