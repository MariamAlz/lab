Arriving at

Walk-out time covers taxiing to the gate, passport control and bags. 30 to 45 minutes is typical; hand luggage only on an e-gate passport can be 20, a full long-haul arrival at peak time can be over an hour.

How the landing time is worked out. Planes broadcast their position, height and speed over ADS-B. This takes the straight-line (great-circle) distance to the runway and splits it into cruise and descent. Pilots plan to descend about 3 nautical miles for every 1,000 ft of height, slowing as they go, so a plane at 38,000 ft starts down roughly 200 km out. The last 30 km of approach and landing are counted as a flat 15 minutes.

What's approximate. The landing time is an estimate that can be off by 10 minutes or more: holding patterns, runway changes and headwinds aren't known. The walk-out and drive times are your guesses. Some airlines fly under a callsign that doesn't match the flight number, and volunteer receivers don't cover every route, so if nothing shows up, use the scheduled time. Flight data comes from adsb.lol, airplanes.live or adsb.fi, and routes from adsbdb. Your location is only used in your browser to estimate the drive.
