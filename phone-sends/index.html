What your phone sends.

Every website you open can quietly read dozens of small facts about your device. None of them is secret on its own, but together they can recognise you again without cookies. Here they are, read live from your device, with how to block each one. This page keeps nothing and sends nothing.

Your device's fingerprint on this browser …

A code made from the facts below. If it stays the same when you come back tomorrow, in a private tab, or after clearing cookies, sites can use it to recognise you.

Seen without asking

Any site and any advert can read these the moment the page loads. No pop-up, no permission.

Seen after you tap "Allow"

These need your permission. Try them: the data stays on this page and disappears when you leave.

    Strongest everyday protection: Safari's "Advanced Tracking and Fingerprinting Protection" (Settings → Apps → Safari → Advanced) or Firefox with "Strict" tracking protection, or the Brave browser, which randomise or hide many of these values. A private tab clears cookies but not your fingerprint.
