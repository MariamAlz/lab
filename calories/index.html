Calorie counter.

Most calorie apps don't know what machboos, harees or a cup of karak is, and they want an account. This one has over 200 foods with plenty of Emirati and Arab dishes, looks up packaged food by barcode, and builds your own recipes. Your log, water and weight stay in this browser.

An estimate, not medical advice. Dish values vary a lot with the cook and the portion. If you're pregnant, under 18, have diabetes, kidney disease or a history of eating disorders, set targets with your doctor or a dietitian.

          Log your weight

            Daily weight swings by 1 to 2 kg with water, salt and food in your stomach. The trend line is an exponentially smoothed average: each day it moves 10% of the way toward the new reading. It shows the real direction weeks before the raw numbers do. Weigh at the same time each morning.

            BMR is the energy you burn at complete rest, from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation: 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age, plus 5 for men or minus 161 for women. Multiplying by your activity gives maintenance. Weight loss targets are capped at a 25% deficit and never go below 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men). Protein is set by body weight, fat at about 28% of calories and carbs fill the rest.

            Your data

            Everything is saved in this browser only. Barcode lookups send just the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database. There's no photo recognition here: guessing a dish's calories from a photo is unreliable, especially for rice dishes.
