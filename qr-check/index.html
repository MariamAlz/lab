QR code safety scanner.

A phone camera opens a QR code the moment it sees one, and you can't read a QR code by eye. Scammers stick fake codes over real ones on parking meters, menus and posters. This scanner never opens anything. It shows what the code would do and flags the tricks first.

Start the camera, or drop a photo or screenshot of a QR code below.

Drop or paste an image here (Ctrl+V works too)
Before you scan in the street
  • Run a finger over the code. A sticker on top of the printed one is the classic parking-meter scam.
  • Pay parking through the official app or SMS number printed on the sign, never through a QR code you found on it.
  • A QR code that asks you to log in, pay a fine or "verify" anything is a red flag.
  • Restaurant menus are usually fine. If a menu code asks for card details, close the page and pay at the till.

Decoding happens on your device using jsQR (Apache 2.0). Nothing you scan is sent anywhere unless you choose "Check the site in depth".
