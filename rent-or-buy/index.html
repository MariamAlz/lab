Rent or buy in Dubai.

Buying a home in the UAE costs about 7 to 8% of the price before you get the keys, and more again when you sell. Renting means paying a landlord, but your cash keeps working. Buying and selling costs · Dubai

The real cost of buying isn't the mortgage interest alone. It's interest, plus fees, plus service charges and upkeep, plus what your down payment would have earned somewhere else. Rent is a cost too, but it's the whole cost. Buying usually wins only if you stay long enough for price growth and the paid-down loan to cover the money spent going in and out. That's the break-even year.

An estimate to help you think, not financial advice. Fees, caps and rates here are approximate, based on rules as generally published. Check the current figures with the Dubai Land Department, Abu Dhabi's ADREC or DARI, your bank and a licensed mortgage adviser before you decide.

Assumptions

In Dubai, rent rises on renewal are limited by the RERA rental index: none if your rent is within 10% of the market average for the area, then up to 5, 10, 15 or 20% the further below it you are. Over many years, rents still track the market.
