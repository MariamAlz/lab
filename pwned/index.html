Has my password leaked?

Billions of passwords from past data breaches are public, and attackers try them first. Check yours against the Have I Been Pwned list without ever sending it: your device turns the password into a fingerprint and shares only its first five characters.

What happens when you press Check
1

Your browser turns the password into a SHA-1 fingerprint, here on your device.…

2

Only the first 5 characters are sent. About a million different passwords share them.…

3

The service sends back every leaked fingerprint that starts that way, plus decoys, so it can't tell which one you wanted.…

4

Your device looks for the rest of your fingerprint in that list.…

This is called k-anonymity, and it's how password managers and browsers check passwords too. Want to know whether your email was in a breach? Check it on haveibeenpwned.com directly; that search needs a key that can't be put safely in a public page.
