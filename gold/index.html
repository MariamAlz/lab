Scholars differ on everyday jewellery: the Hanafi school counts it, while most Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali scholars exempt jewellery worn normally. Untick to leave it out, and ask a scholar you trust if unsure.

Type what the shop quoted for each piece. Add more pieces to compare them, or to check a whole set.

Typical Dubai making charges, per gram of gold: plain chains AED 8–25, plain bangles 10–35, pieces set with stones 30–120, branded or designer 60–300 and up. Gold Souk prices sit at the low end; malls and brands at the high end.

Rates are worked out from the international spot price, converted at the dirham's fixed peg (3.6725 to the dollar). UAE shop boards are usually within about 1% of this; type your shop's 24K rate above to match it exactly. A tola is 11.66 g. Nothing you enter leaves this page.
