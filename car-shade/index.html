Will my car be in the shade?

You found a shady spot at 9 am. By noon the shadow has moved and your steering wheel is too hot to touch. Mark where you parked and this shows, for every 10 minutes of today, whether the spot is in the shadow of a building or a tree, and when that changes.

    How it works. For each 10-minute step it finds where the sun is, then casts every nearby building's shadow along the ground: a building of height h throws a shadow h ÷ tan(sun altitude) long, pointing away from the sun. It checks five points around your car. Building heights come from OpenStreetMap, or floors × 3.2 m, or 3 floors if nothing is mapped. Trees are taken as 6 m tall with a 5 m crown unless mapped.

    This is an estimate. It doesn't know about car park roofs, shade sails, parking canopies or trees that aren't on the map, so a covered spot will look sunnier here than it is.
