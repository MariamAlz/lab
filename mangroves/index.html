Mangrove watch.

One clear Sentinel-2 picture from every autumn since 2017, over Abu Dhabi's eastern mangroves and Mangrove National Park. Plants reflect near-infrared light strongly, so a simple index, NDVI, shows how green each 10 m pixel is.

The UAE has pledged to plant 100 million mangroves by 2030. Mangroves store carbon in their mud, shelter young fish and protect the coast from waves. "Green area" here counts every pixel with NDVI above 0.3 inside this box, so parks and lawns count too, and the tide and season nudge it from year to year.
