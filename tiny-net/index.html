Put down blue and pink dots, press train, and watch a small network learn where one colour ends and the other begins. The background shows what it believes about every point on the board.

With no hidden layers the network can only draw one straight line, so XOR is impossible for it. Add a layer and it can bend the boundary. Each neuron is just a weighted sum squashed through a curve; learning means nudging every weight a little downhill on the loss, over and over (backpropagation).
