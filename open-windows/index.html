Open the windows or AC?

From November to March, UAE evenings and early mornings are often cooler outside than inside. Opening the windows then airs the house for free. This checks every hour of the next two days for cool air, dry air, clean air and no dust, and tells you when the next good window is.

    A 1.5 ton split unit draws about 1.5 to 2 kW. Check your bill for your real price per kWh, which depends on your emirate and how much you use.

    Why the dew point matters more than humidity. Air at 20°C and 80% humidity sounds cool, but its dew point is 16.5°C. Air at 26°C and 70% has a dew point near 20°C. Let that in and your sofa, walls and bedding soak up the moisture, and when you close up again the AC spends its first hour drying the house instead of cooling it. Below about 18°C dew point, outside air feels fresh. Above it, it feels sticky, even if it's cooler.

    What's approximate. The weather and air quality are forecasts from Open-Meteo and the CAMS model, not a sensor on your street. A busy road, a building site or a neighbour's generator can make your air worse than the model. The energy figure assumes the AC would otherwise run about 40% of each hour; your real saving depends on your home and settings.
