What your commute costs.

Fuel, Salik and parking add up quietly. Enter your daily trip once and compare driving, the Dubai Metro or bus, a taxi and Etihad Rail side by side: money per month and per year, time on the road, and CO2. Every price is editable, so you can match your own receipts.

The cheapest option on paper is often not the one people pick. A quick way to compare fairly: put a value on your time. If an hour of your time is worth 50 AED to you, 20 extra minutes each way over 22 days is about 730 AED a month, which can close the gap between the metro and the car.

An estimate to help you compare, not a quote. Fares, tolls and fuel prices change. Check RTA, Salik and Etihad Rail for current prices.

October 2026 UAE retail prices (UAE Fuel Price Committee, via Khaleej Times): Super 98 4.40, Special 95 4.28, E-Plus 4.21, Diesel 4.80. They change on the 1st of each month.

Dubai variable Salik, in force since 31 January 2025: AED 6 on weekdays 6–10am and 4–8pm, AED 4 at other times (and all day on Sundays), free 1–6am. Different hours apply in Ramadan. Abu Dhabi Darb gates are AED 4 at peak (7–9am, 3–7pm) and free otherwise: set the peak price to 4 and the off-peak to 0.

RTA nol fares, checked October 2026: Silver AED 3 / 5 / 7.50 for 1 / 2 / more zones, Gold Class double. 30-day Silver pass AED 140 / 230 / 350 (1 zone / 2 zones / all zones). Students and senior citizens pay half on a personal card. Picking zones and card fills these in; you can then edit them.

Dubai RTA taxis (Nov 2025 revision): AED 5 flag-fall by day, about AED 2.14–2.26 per km (set monthly with fuel prices), AED 12 minimum on the street, AED 13 and a 4 to 7.50 booking fee when booked in an app. Careem and Uber prices vary with demand: edit to match your usual receipt.

An estimate. Passenger fares are not widely published yet. Press reports in September 2026 gave Dubai (Al Yalayis) to Abu Dhabi from about AED 39 in Comfort, about 64 minutes, with few trains a day and only a handful of stations open. Edit the fare to match your ticket. It only makes sense for intercity commutes such as Dubai to Abu Dhabi.
