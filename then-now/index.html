Then and now.

The same patch of the UAE seen from orbit in 2000 and this month. Drag across the picture to wipe between them.

⇆

The 2000 images come from Landsat 7, which sees the ground in 30 m pixels; today's are Sentinel-2 at 10 m, which is why the old ones look softer. Both are real surface reflectance, colour-balanced the same way.

Landsat imagery courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel-2 data.
