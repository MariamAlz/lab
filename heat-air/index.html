    How the risk is worked out

    Heat stress uses the wet-bulb temperature, which is what a wet cloth would cool to. It captures humidity, and it's why 38°C on the coast in August can be worse than 45°C in Al Ain. Above about 28°C wet-bulb, the body struggles to cool itself during exercise. Above 31°C, even resting outdoors is hard work.

    Air uses the US AQI from fine particles (PM2.5), coarse dust (PM10) and ozone. On dusty days, PM10 can jump to ten times its normal level within an hour. UV of 8 or more burns unprotected skin in under 20 minutes.

    Outdoor workers in the UAE get a midday break from 12:30 to 15:00 between 15 June and 15 September each year. If you employ a driver, gardener or domestic worker, the strip above shows why. Data from Open-Meteo forecasts (CAMS for air quality), so it's modelled rather than read from a nearby sensor. If you feel dizzy, confused or stop sweating, get cool and call 998 or 999.
