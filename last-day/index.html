    Summary

    The law lets the contract set notice between 30 and 90 days. Your contract or offer letter says which.
    Working it out assumes no leave was lost along the way. Many contracts limit how many unused days carry over to the next year, so if HR gives you a balance, use that.

    How leave builds up under the law: nothing is owed for the first six months, then 2 days for each month worked until the first year, then 30 days a year, counted pro rata for part of a year. Unused days you haven't taken are paid out on your basic salary when you leave.

    Also worth knowing: if one side skips the notice period, it owes the other the pay for the notice days not worked. On probation, different notice rules apply. Final dues, including your end-of-service gratuity, are due within 14 days of the contract ending. This is an estimate, not legal advice: check your contract and MOHRE (80060), and free-zone or government jobs may follow other rules.
