Shady walk.

Map apps find the shortest walk. On a hot afternoon you'd happily walk a bit further to stay out of the sun. Pick a start and an end, and this works out where the building shadows fall at that time and finds the route with the most shade.

    How it works. A building of height h casts a shadow h ÷ tan(sun altitude) long, pointing away from the sun. At 4 pm in Dubai the sun is about 25° up, so a 10 m villa throws a 21 m shadow and a 100 m tower more than 200 m. The route finder treats a sunny metre as costing more than a shady one (1 + your setting), then picks the cheapest path.

    Building outlines, heights and paths come from OpenStreetMap. Where a height is missing it uses floors × 3.2 m, or 3 floors. Trees, arcades and shade sails are not counted, and many buildings are mapped roughly, so treat it as a good guess. Keep the start and end within about 2 km.
