What a stranger can learn from your profile.

One post says little. A month of posts says where you live, when you're out and what your security answers are. Describe what you posted publicly in the last month, and see the dossier a patient stranger could build. Nothing is looked up or scraped. Everything stays in this browser and nothing is uploaded.
