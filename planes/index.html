What plane is that?

Planes broadcast their position, height and speed every second over a radio signal called ADS-B, and volunteers around the world receive it. This page asks those receivers what's near you and shows which flight is overhead, where it's going and how high it is. Point your phone at a plane to label it.

Live positions come from community receiver networks (adsb.lol, airplanes.live, adsb.fi or OpenSky, whichever answers), and routes come from adsbdb. Military and some private flights don't broadcast, and a route can be wrong when an airline reuses a flight number. Your location is used only to ask for nearby planes.
