Every message here is invented. Bank, courier and company names are fictional, and any real authority names appear only because scammers pretend to be them. No real logos are used.

The one habit that beats nearly every trick: don't act from the message. If it is about your bank, a fine, a parcel or your boss, open the official app or call a number you already have. Phishing works by keeping you inside the scammer's story.
