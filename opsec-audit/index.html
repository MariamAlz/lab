Personal OPSEC audit.

OPSEC means thinking like someone who wants to find you, then closing the gaps. Most leaks are small and innocent: a school logo in a photo, a Wi-Fi name, a story posted while you're still at the café. Answer 37 quick questions to see where you're exposed, and get a fix plan in order of importance. Your answers stay in this browser.

    Your exposure by area
    –Answer a few questions to see your score.

    How the score works. Each answer scores 0, 1 or 2, weighted by how much harm the gap could do (1 to 3). An area's score is the share of the best possible points you have, ignoring questions that don't apply to you. The fix plan puts the heaviest, worst-scoring items first.

    Why it matters. Stalkers, scammers and burglars rarely hack anything. They piece together public crumbs: a car plate from one post, a school from another, a holiday from a third. Each crumb is harmless alone. Together they answer "where, when and who".
