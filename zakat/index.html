The hawl, one lunar year

Zakat is due when your wealth has stayed above the nisab for a full Hijri year. Pick the day it first reached the nisab, or the date you pay each year.

Good to know

Zakat is 2.5% of wealth you have held for a full lunar year, once it is above the nisab. The nisab is the value of 85 g of gold or 595 g of silver. Silver gives a much lower threshold, so more people pass it. Many scholars prefer it for cash because it benefits the poor more, while others use gold because it is closer to today's value of money.

Gold is valued by its pure content: 21K jewellery is 21/24 gold, so 100 g of 21K counts as 87.5 g of pure gold. Your home, car, furniture and work tools are not zakatable. If you pay on a Gregorian date instead of a Hijri one, some scholars suggest 2.577% to make up the 11 extra days.

This is a calculator, not a fatwa. For your own situation, especially business, shares, debts or crypto, ask a scholar you trust or the official fatwa service in the UAE.
