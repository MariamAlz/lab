How the verdicts work

Waves here are the significant wave height: the average of the biggest third of waves. About one wave in a hundred is nearly twice that size. Period is the time between waves. Long-period swell, which reaches the east coast from the Arabian Sea, carries more power than its height suggests.

Wind direction matters as much as speed. Onshore wind (from the sea) brings chop but pushes you back to the beach. Offshore wind (from the land) looks calm near the shore but can blow a paddleboard, kite or swimmer out to sea, so the verdicts get stricter. In winter, the shamal wind from the north-west can rise quickly on the Gulf coast.

These are model forecasts from Open-Meteo, not readings from a buoy at the beach, and a guide rather than safety advice. Swim at beaches with lifeguards, follow the beach flags and coast guard warnings, and check with your boat operator. In an emergency call 999.
