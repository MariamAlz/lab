Don't name your Wi-Fi after your home

Your network name is broadcast to the street, the corridor and every phone that walks past. "Villa 23" or "The Hassan Family" tells a stranger which network belongs to which door, and that the family inside uses it.

    Neutral ideas:

    Falcon-5GBlue LanternSidr TreeKettle Net

    Hiding the name doesn't add real security, and your phone then calls out for it everywhere you go.

    Why a separate network helps: devices on the same Wi-Fi can usually see each other. If a cheap plug or camera is taken over, a separate guest or IoT network keeps it away from the laptops and phones where your banking and photos live.

    General guidance. Menu names differ by brand, so check your device's manual or app. If your internet provider manages your router, ask them about settings you can't change.
