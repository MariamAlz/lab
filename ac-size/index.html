AC size and running cost.

Too small and the AC runs flat out all August without cooling the room. Too big and it short-cycles, leaving the air cold but damp. Describe your room to get a size in BTU and tons, then see what it costs to run each month on your emirate's tariff, and how much an inverter or a warmer thermostat saves.

    Month by month

    How the size is worked out: a base of 650 BTU per hour for each square metre of floor at a 2.7 m ceiling (about 1 ton per 18 m², a common Gulf rule of thumb; cooler climates use about a third of that). Then: more for sun on the walls and a roof above, 600 BTU for each person beyond two, about 1,000 BTU per sunny window, 4,000 for a kitchen, and 3.4 BTU for each watt of appliances. Real installers also check insulation, glass type and duct losses, so treat this as a starting point to compare quotes.

    The room

    Appliances: a TV about 100 W, a gaming PC 300–500 W, a fridge 150 W, lights 10 W per LED bulb.

    Running it

    EER 9.5 is typical for a fixed-speed (non-inverter) split unit at Gulf summer temperatures. An inverter runs at part speed most of the time instead of stopping and starting, which typically uses 30–40% less over a season; the comparison uses 35%. The thermostat effect is a rule of thumb: published figures range from about 3% to 10% per degree, so edit it if you have a better number.
