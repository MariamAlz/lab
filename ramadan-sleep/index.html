Ramadan sleep planner.

In Ramadan, sleep gets cut in two by suhoor and Fajr, and the evening fills up with iftar and Taraweeh. Waking in the middle of a sleep cycle is what leaves you groggy. This fits whole 90-minute cycles around your prayer times, work and commute, as one core block plus naps.

    Tips

      Sleep runs in cycles of roughly 90 minutes, from light sleep to deep sleep to dreaming and back. Waking at the end of a cycle feels far easier than waking from deep sleep. Cycle length varies from about 80 to 110 minutes between people, so treat 90 as a starting point and adjust by 10 minutes if you wake groggy.

      Prayer times use the UAE method by default (Fajr and Isha when the sun is 18.2° below the horizon, Maghrib at sunset). Umm al-Qura uses 18.5° for Fajr and puts Isha 90 minutes after Maghrib, or 2 hours in Ramadan. They are calculated, not taken from an official timetable, and can differ by a few minutes from your mosque or the official UAE app. Ramadan dates use the Umm al-Qura calendar in your browser; in the UAE the start is confirmed by moon sighting. This is a planning aid, not medical advice. If you have diabetes or another condition, check fasting and sleep plans with your doctor.
